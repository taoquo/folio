<h1 align="center">Folio Drawing DSL V4.3</h1>

<p align="center">Three production notation grammars complete the seventeen-type registry.</p>

### Changelog

1. Added a coordinate-free Sequence grammar with bounded participants, ordered sync/async/return messages, stable ids, lifelines, and distinct message marks.
2. Migrated UML Class into the shared compiler, scene, profile, accessibility, host, catalog, and artifact pipeline while retaining the legacy loader as a compatibility facade.
3. Added an ER Diagram grammar with bounded entities and fields, explicit keys and nullability, cardinality-bearing relationships, and accessible field descriptions.
4. Added authoritative detailed schemas plus minimal and canonical fixtures for Sequence, UML Class, and ER Diagram.
5. Added fail-closed notation gates for duplicate relationships, unknown endpoints, nullable primary keys, density, text fit, item overlap, detached routes, non-orthogonal routes, and unrelated-item crossings.
6. Expanded registry, schema, catalog, CLI, CI, package, documentation, and host coverage from fourteen to seventeen generator-backed types.
7. Approved a reviewed seventeen-diagram visual baseline and passed the 153-artifact profile/format matrix with deterministic seventeen-fixture batch replay.
8. Recorded a Sankey no-go for V4.3 until a distinct editorial job and deterministic conservation, routing, crossing, and label-fit prototype meet the production bar.

### 更新日志

1. 新增无坐标时序图语法，限制参与者与消息数量，支持同步、异步和返回消息，提供稳定 ID、生命线和可区分的消息标记。
2. 将 UML 类图迁入共享编译器、场景、Profile、可访问性、宿主、目录和 artifact 管线，同时保留旧加载器作为兼容入口。
3. 新增 ER 图语法，支持有界实体与字段、显式主外键与可空性、带基数的关系以及可访问字段描述。
4. 为时序图、UML 类图和 ER 图新增权威详细 Schema、最小样例和标准样例。
5. 新增失败闭合记法门禁，覆盖重复关系、未知端点、可空主键、密度、文本适配、节点重叠、端点脱离、非正交路径和穿越无关节点。
6. 将注册表、Schema、目录、CLI、CI、打包、文档和宿主覆盖从十四种扩展到十七种生成器驱动图表。
7. 批准经过人工审查的十七图视觉基线，并通过 153 产物 Profile/格式矩阵与十七样例确定性批处理复放。
8. 为 V4.3 记录 Sankey 暂不实施决策，等待独立编辑价值以及守恒、路由、交叉和标签适配原型达到生产门槛。
